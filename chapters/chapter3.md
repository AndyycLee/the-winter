\## Chapter 3\\. December

It wasn't actually the first time I had seen her. I had seen her before, but it had been many years and I doubt she remembered me. We were both in the same class in elementary school, and I had been a quiet, awkward boy who couldn't speak English and spent most of his time in the library. She had been there too, but she was also bright and lively, always surrounded by friends and laughter.

Now, years later, here she was, sitting on the bench in the snow.
